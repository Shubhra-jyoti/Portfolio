# Personal Portfolio

This repository contains the source code for my personal portfolio website. 

In the modern digital landscape, a portfolio is more than just a collection of past work. It is a direct reflection of an individual's professional identity. It serves as a central hub where technical capability meets personal branding. Relying solely on a standard resume is often insufficient to capture the nuance of software development, design decisions, and problem-solving skills. 

By building and maintaining this portfolio, I have a dedicated space to take control of my professional narrative. It allows me to demonstrate my technical proficiency in a real-world environment and provides a tangible look at what I can bring to future teams and projects. The ability to present oneself effectively is crucial, and this platform is designed to ensure that anyone reviewing my work has a clear, accessible, and well-structured way to understand my capabilities.

This project handles both client-side presentation and server-side logic to deliver a seamless application experience, serving as a living document of my continuous growth as a developer.
